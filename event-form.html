Make the case

Name the actual group this matters most to — a major, a class year, students job-hunting this semester — and why the timing makes it relevant right now rather than any other week. This is what earns the event a spot on someone's calendar.
Picture a student who's never been to a Career Services event and knows nothing about this topic. What's the one concrete thing they walk away with — a skill, an answer, a next step? Not "learns about interviews" — what specifically changes for them by the end.
Flag anything the designer should frame carefully rather than word casually — topics tied to grief, mental health, job loss, or financial hardship; religious, political, or cultural content; comparisons that could read as favoring one major, nationality, or group over another; a speaker or sponsor with any public controversy attached; or content aimed at a specific identity group that needs to be worded with care. If in doubt, flag it here rather than after the poster's already live.
