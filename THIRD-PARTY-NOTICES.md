## Notes on compliance

Both fonts are embedded as base64 inside `portable.html`. Because that data can be
extracted, this is treated as redistribution rather than mere embedding, and the
full licence text plus copyright notice is included accordingly.

Neither typeface declares a Reserved Font Name, and neither has been modified —
the `.woff2` files are the originals as published. The fonts are not sold
separately, and no other licence is applied to them.

The copyright strings in the font binaries' internal metadata differ slightly
from the strings in the licence files as published (the binaries read "Copyright
2021 Kasper Nordkvist" and "Copyright 2024 The Geist Project Authors"). The
notices reproduced above are taken from the licence files that accompany each
release, which are the operative notices for OFL clause 2.

No other third-party code, libraries, icons, or assets are used. All remaining
code is original and covered by `LICENSE`.
